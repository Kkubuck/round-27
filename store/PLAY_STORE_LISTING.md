## Full description

Round 27 is a clean AMRAP timer built around Cindy.

Set your time cap and round goal, start the clock, and tap once after every completed round. Round 27 automatically recalculates the pace you need for every remaining round, so you can see whether you are ahead or behind without doing math during the workout.

Features:

- Cindy setup: 5 pull-ups, 10 push-ups and 15 squats
- 27 Round Challenge preset
- Dynamic target pace after every round
- Large 20-minute countdown and round counter
- Undo, pause and protected reset controls
- 1080×1920 result card for Instagram Stories
- Native share sheet, haptics and screen-awake mode
- Fully offline with no account required

Train at your own level. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual discomfort.
